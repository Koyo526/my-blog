## Thoughts Lately
Doesn't the title "Tech Lead" sound cool?
I don't know the specifics of what the job entails, though...

Due to an organizational change, I've had more opportunities to work alongside a Frontend Tech Lead. Seeing them share technical insights gave me the impression that it's a really cool, "engineer-like" role.
(Of course, they do more than just share information; they also handle technical decision-making and provide technical support.)

I had previously thought I wanted to go into management in the future, but now I'm starting to feel like the Tech Lead role is a great option, too.


## Learning Java
I earned the Java Silver certification last year before the autumn JJUG CCC, and I aim to get Java Gold this year (likely around August or September).
It is less about simply obtaining the certification and more about maintaining the motivation to study (though, to be honest, vendor certifications are expensive, so it requires some financial commitment).
Lately, I’ve been planning to work through the "Black Book" (comprehensive study guide) for Java Gold on a daily basis.

[Thorough Guide: Java SE 17 Gold Question Bank](https://amzn.asia/d/01omALkY)

## Architecture
I also want to advance my studies in architecture.
I have a strong desire to get involved starting from the higher-level decision-making stages.
The term "FDE" (Forward Development Engineer) has been gaining traction recently; I believe it requires the drive to handle the entire process—from deeply analyzing the core of a problem to building the actual product.
I bought a book titled *The Architect's Textbook*, so I plan to read that as well.

[The Architect's Textbook: Building Software Architecture That Creates Value](https://amzn.asia/d/01MIuwHe)
